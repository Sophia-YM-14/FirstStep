Prepare for an Interview

Walk Into Your Interview Prepared

An interview is a chance for you and the employer to learn more about each other. Preparation can help you communicate clearly and feel more confident.

Do

  • Research the company.
  • Arrive prepared.
  • Listen carefully.
  • Speak clearly.
  • Be honest.
  • Ask thoughtful questions.
  • Thank the interviewer.

Don’t

  • Arrive unprepared.
  • Interrupt the interviewer.
  • Pretend to know something you don't.Pretend to know something.
  • Memorize every answer word-for-word.Memorize answers word-for-word.
  • Speak negatively about previous experiences.Speak negatively about past roles.

Common Interview Questions

"Tell me about yourself."

Give a short introduction about your education, relevant experience, skills, and career interests.Give a short introduction about your education, experience, and career interests. Tip: Keep your answer related to the position.

Tip: Keep your answer focused on information related to the position.

"Why are you interested in this position?"

Explain what interests you about the role and how it connects with your skills or goals.

"What are your strengths?"

Choose strengths that are relevant to the job and support your answer with a simple example.

"What is something you want to improve?"

Choose an area you are genuinely working on and explain how you are improving it.

"Why should we hire you?"

Talk about the skills, attitude, and willingness to learn that you can bring to the position.
